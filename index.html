Freight businesses can win revenue while losing contribution, and profitable work can still strain cash when carrier bills fall due before customer collections. This prototype brings those questions into one browser-based workspace.

Project overview

Proposed solution for logistics teams

Problem
Disconnected quoting, shipment cost and cash planning can hide weak margins and timing risks.
What it does
Provides educational shipment costing, carrier comparison, liquidity planning and scenario analysis tools.
Target users
Freight forwarders, transport operators, importers/exporters, logistics managers and pricing teams.
Service area
India-focused sample context; the browser demo has no live operating territory.
Value proposition
Brings shipment contribution and payment timing into one clear decision workspace.
Technology
HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No account, API or backend required.
Data
All shipment records are illustrative and stored locally in this browser session.

Contact / feedback

This static demo cannot send form data to a server.

Demo contact identity: no real company email or address has been configured. Email draft opens your configured mail app and is not sent automatically.
